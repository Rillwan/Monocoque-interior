FAQ

Common Questions

What services does Monocoque Interiors offer?

We provide residential and commercial construction, structural and civil works, interior design and finishing, renovation and remodeling, and turnkey projects.

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. Design, construction, interiors and finishing are managed by one team, from concept and planning to handover.

How does a project start?

Every project begins with a consultation to understand your requirements, followed by conceptual and architectural design and a detailed estimate.

How is the budget decided?

We prepare a detailed estimate and budget before execution begins, so scope and costs are clear before work starts.

Can you renovate a home or workspace that is in use?

It depends on the scope. We plan the work and its sequence with you during consultation to keep disruption as low as possible.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Kochi, Kerala, and serve clients in 5+ cities. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.
